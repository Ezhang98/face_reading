A note on face reading. Mianxiang (面相) is a folk tradition from Chinese culture, over two thousand years old, offered here as cultural exploration and entertainment. It has no scientific basis — facial features do not determine character, fortune, or destiny. Enjoy it the way you'd enjoy a tea-leaf reading.
